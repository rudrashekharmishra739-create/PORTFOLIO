About

Solene is a fictional fine-dining concept created for a web-design portfolio. It imagines a small restaurant with a short menu, a tight room and the confidence to slow down.

Everything begins with what's in season — farmers, market stalls and the weather decide the menu. We cook honestly, plate simply and let the produce speak.

  • Local first — produce sourced within a day's drive
  • Short menu — six courses, changed often
  • No rushes — dinner is an evening, not a table flip
  • Quiet service — attentive, never hovering
03 / 06

Our Chef

Chef · Concept persona

Aditi Rao

A fictional head chef for the concept. Aditi's style is simple on the plate and exacting in the prep — fire, salt and timing over tricks and garnish.

The kitchen she leads runs a tight, seasonal menu that changes with the market, written on a single board each morning.
