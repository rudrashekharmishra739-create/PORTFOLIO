Concept Project 01 / 06 — Form / Space

Courtyard House

A house organised around a planted central court. Every room opens onto light, air and a moment of stillness, while the street stays at a courteous distance.

LocationKanpur, Uttar Pradesh
CategoryResidential
Year2026 · Concept
Area4,200 sq ft
StatusFictional concept
Concept

Design Concept

The house is planned as a closed shell with an open heart. A low, solid wall holds the street at the front; inside, a single court becomes the daylight source, the air shaft and the social centre of the home.

All living areas face the court rather than the road. Tall shaded openings temper the north Indian summer, while the court's planted well cools incoming air before it reaches the rooms. Bedrooms sit above, reached by a staircase that reads as a horizontal cast-stone band.

Materials stay deliberately simple — fair-faced concrete, lime plaster and reclaimed red brick — so the architecture reads as mass, light and shade rather than decoration.

A house that breathes through its middle.
