The Retreat

TERRA is a fictional boutique retreat concept created for a web-design portfolio. It imagines a small hillside hotel where rooms are few, windows are wide and nothing is over-animated.

The daily menu comes from the kitchen garden. The staff know your name by the second day. There is no valet, no lobby drama — just calm, done carefully.
