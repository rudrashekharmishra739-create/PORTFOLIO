About NOIR

Modern grooming & personal expression.

NOIR & CO. is a fictional premium salon concept created for a web-design portfolio. The space it imagines is built around personal expression — a comfortable, unisex studio where modern cuts, careful grooming and honest styling advice come first.

We want every visit to feel relaxed and considered: no rush, no templates, just the small details that make a cut look intentional. Prices and team shown here are concept content, not real business claims.
