Studio

Design begins with understanding.

FORM / SPACE is a fictional architecture and interior design studio built as a portfolio concept. We work slowly and deliberately, grounding every decision in context, function and material.

Context The site, climate and city
Function How a space is actually used
Materials Honest, enduring, tactile
Light Daylight as the first material
Proportion Calm, balanced, human
Human experience How the space feels
