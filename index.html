FAQ

Questions, answered

The things people usually ask before we start.

What do I need before I build a website or app?

Just a clear idea of what you want to achieve. Copy, images and branding can come later — structure, content and goals are enough to begin.

Can you turn my idea into an MVP?

Yes. The aim is the first working version of your product, built to test demand and learn from real users before investing more.

Can you redesign an existing website or app?

Yes. We keep what works — content, URLs, accounts — and rebuild the experience so it loads faster and works better on phones.

How long does it take to build a website or app?

A marketing site typically takes 1–3 weeks, a web store 3–6 weeks, and an MVP or web app depends on scope. You get a timeline before work starts.

Who owns the code, design and accounts?

You do. Everything is handed over when the project finishes, and you get the code and the guides to run it yourself.

How do you make a website easier to find on Google and AI search?

Clean semantics, fast performance, structured data and real content. The same fundamentals that help Google also help AI search.

What happens after launch?

You get a short support window, and optional monthly care — updates, backups and improvements — if you want to keep it maintained.
