How We Roast

01

Buy green

Small lots bought direct from growers we can name, cupped before they leave the farm.

02

Roast gently

A slow drum, careful heat and constant cupping until the coffee tastes like itself.

03

Rest & release

Beans rest for three days, then hit the shelf with a roast date you can read.

04

Fresh only

If a bag sits past two weeks, we pull it and give it to the cafe's batch brew.

03 / 06

The Story

KILN is a fictional coffee roastery concept created for a web-design portfolio. It imagines a two-person roastery operating out of a small warehouse in Indiranagar.

No blends for the sake of it, no names that promise more than the cup — just green coffee treated gently and sent out fast while it still tastes alive.

The cafe counter sits beside the drum, so the roast of the week always has a brew bar next to it.
